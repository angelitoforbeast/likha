# Brief 185: Speed screen charts — average, With AI vs No AI, and pushed → sent at Meta

## Why
The Speed screen has the numbers per hour in tables (reply time with average/median/p90/max; With AI vs No AI; sequences pushed → sent at Meta) but only one chart (median and p90 of the reply time). The owner reads charts to see at a glance which hour got slow, wants the **average** visible, wants **With AI vs No AI** as a chart, and wants to see the **delay between Likha handing a reply to the sender and Meta's confirmation that it was sent**, per hour, for bot replies (not only sequences), so he can tell whether a slow hour was Likha (flow/AI) or the send path (Forbeast/Meta).
